Research Overview

Exploring machine learning solutions for sustainable rice farming

Optimizing Nitrogen Fertilizer Application in Paddies

Our research focuses on developing an accurate, handy, and affordable nitrogen management system for smallholder rice farmers using machine learning with a mobile application for leaf color detection.

The Problem

Traditional nitrogen monitoring techniques like the Leaf Color Chart (LCC) are subjective and prone to human error, while precision equipment like SPAD chlorophyll meters are too expensive for smallholder farmers.

Our Solution

A mobile application that automatically detects nitrogen deficiency in rice leaves using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) based on standard LCC guidelines, with 98% classification accuracy.

Key Research Components

Machine Learning Model

We developed a pre-optimized DenseNet121 Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model trained using thousands of annotated images of rice leaves. The model categorizes leaf images into four nitrogen status categories with 98% accuracy.

  • Severe deficiency
  • Moderate deficiency
  • Mild deficiency
  • Sufficient nitrogen status

Mobile Application

The mobile app is built on the Flutter platform, making it cross-platform compatible and lightweight for low-end Android devices. It delivers results within two seconds and provides specific fertilizer recommendations based on the analysis.

Key features include:

  • Real-time leaf color analysis
  • Location-specific fertilizer recommendations
  • Offline functionality for rural areas
  • User-friendly interface for farmers

Field Testing & Validation

We conducted extensive field trials in Sri Lanka's Western Province rice-growing regions, collecting 1,082 leaf images under various conditions. The system was tested on 15 different models of Android devices to ensure hardware tolerance.

Field results showed:

  • 15% improvement in nitrogen-use efficiency
  • 70% reduction in device-dependent variability
  • High farmer acceptance and usability

About the Project

This groundbreaking research was conducted at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura's Faculty of Technology, Department of Information & Communication Technology. Our team developed an innovative machine learning system to optimize nitrogen fertilizer application in rice paddies through leaf color analysis. The project combines precision agriculture with mobile technology to create sustainable solutions for smallholder farmers.
