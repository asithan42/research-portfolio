  • Early stopping to prevent overfitting

Conclusion & Future Work

Research Impact

Our research successfully demonstrated how machine learning and mobile technology can enhance nitrogen management in rice cultivation, particularly for smallholder farmers. The system achieved:

  • High accuracy (98%) surpassing traditional methods
  • Practical usability on low-end smartphones
  • Measurable improvements in nitrogen-use efficiency
  • Positive reception from target users

Future Directions

While the results are promising, future work could explore:

  • Expansion to iOS platform for broader accessibility
  • Integration of other nutrient deficiency detection (phosphorus, potassium)
  • Incorporation of real-time environmental data (soil moisture, temperature)
  • Development of regional-specific models for different rice varieties
  • Commercialization pathways for wider adoption
Potential directions for future research

About the Project

This groundbreaking research was conducted at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura's Faculty of Technology, Department of Information & Communication Technology. Our team developed an innovative machine learning system to optimize nitrogen fertilizer application in rice paddies through leaf color analysis. The project combines precision agriculture with mobile technology to create sustainable solutions for smallholder farmers.
