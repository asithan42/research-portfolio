About the Project

Revolutionizing nitrogen management in rice farming through AI

Project Overview

Our research focuses on developing an accurate, handy, and affordable nitrogen management system for smallholder rice farmers using machine learning for leaf color detection. Nitrogen is one of the principal nutrients for rice farming, involved in many functions like the synthesis of chlorophyll, vegetative growth, photosynthesis, and grain production.

However, mismanagement of nitrogen fertilizer either in the form of underapplication or overapplication has far-reaching consequences on agriculture and the environment. Nitrogen deficiency causes weak plants and reduced yields, while overapplication causes water pollution, greenhouse gas emission, and high production costs.

Problems & Our Solutions

Challenges

  • Farmers traditionally depend on tools like the Leaf Color Chart (LCC) which are susceptible to human error and inconsistency
  • Precision solutions like SPAD meters are too expensive for smallholder farmers
  • Existing digital methods fail to account for technical variability in real-world conditions
  • Poor nitrogen management affects regional food production and environmental quality

Our Innovation

  • Mobile application based on machine learning for nitrogen deficiency detection
  • Pre-optimized DenseNet121 CNN model with 98% classification accuracy
  • Robust image preprocessing pipeline for varying lighting conditions
  • Cross-platform Flutter app optimized for low-end smartphones
  • Real-time results with specific fertilizer recommendations

Project Scope

Focused Implementation

  • The research is exclusively on rice cultivation with particular emphasis on widely cultivated varieties in Sri Lanka. The intended user group is small-scale farmers who generally cultivate less than two hectares of land.

    • Technical Specifications

    • Input: Images of leaves captured using regular smartphone cameras
    • Processing: Works on both high-end and low-cost mobile phones
    • Output: Nitrogen deficiency class and precise fertilizer recommendations
    • Recommendations based on plant growth stage and local soil types

    Future Potential

  • While currently focused on nitrogen management, the design allows for easy future expansion to other nutrient deficiencies.

    • Research Objectives

    Technical Objective 1

    Construction of a high-precision CNN model that can classify leaf images into four nitrogen status categories at over 95% accuracy.

    Technical Objective 2

    Creation of an intelligent recommendation system that interprets classification outcomes into usable fertilizer recommendations.

    Technical Objective 3

    Development of an intuitive mobile app that is performance-optimized for low-end smartphones common in target user groups.

    Significance of the Research

    Theoretical Contribution

    Adds to the body of literature on uses of machine learning in agriculture in real-world settings, specifically on tackling problems of variability in field conditions.

    Practical Impact

    Demonstrates implementation of high-level technology into resource-constrained situations, serving as a model for other agricultural applications.

    Social Benefits

    Helps bridge the precision agriculture divide between smallholder and large-scale commercial farmers.

    Environmental Benefits

    Widespread adoption could lead to reduced fertilizer runoff and associated pollution.

    Economic Benefits

    Improves farm-level profitability by increasing efficiency of input utilization while providing affordable accessibility.
